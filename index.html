A Journey of Flavors

At Habesha Restaurant, we bring the rich culinary traditions of Ethiopia and Eritrea to Dresden. Our dishes are meant to be shared, fostering a sense of community and togetherness. From our freshly baked Injera to our rich, aromatic stews, every bite is a celebration of authentic flavors.

Menu Highlights

Zigni Dorho

Tender chicken simmered in a spicy and aromatic berbere sauce, served with hard-boiled eggs.

Vegan & Vegetarian Delights

A colorful selection of vegetable and lentil stews including Allicha, Shiro, and Hamli, perfect for plant-based diets.

Tibsi Beggie

Savory chunks of pan-fried lamb with onions, garlic, and fresh green chilies.

* All our main dishes are served with traditional Injera flatbread.
